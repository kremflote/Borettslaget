Two more patterns:
- **Priorities shift during the search** (15 / 21). Sun, storage and outdoor space are what people "discover" (R05, R06, R08, R09, R15).
- **Price differences must be explained** (16 of 19 who answered). Without a reason, people feel cheated: "Hvis jeg ikke forstår det, føler jeg meg litt lurt." (R05)

## Megler, chatbot and personal data

- **Contacted the megler because the site didn't answer:** 9 / 21. Usual questions: fellesutgifter, sun, why one unit costs more, parking, technical details, what will be built nearby.
- **Four first-time buyers avoid the megler** because it feels embarrassing or pushy (R03 R07 R14 R20). Three of them said they'd rather ask a chatbot for that reason.
- **Would trust a chatbot about a unit:** yes 6, conditionally 12, no 3. The condition is almost always "facts only, and show the source" (7 say so outright, e.g. "dette står i leveransebeskrivelsen, side 12").
- **Would share needs, household or a budget range for suggestions:** 17 / 20 who answered. **Would share income, equity or ID number:** 0. Several don't want their contact details or max budget to reach the megler.

## Megler interview (1)

- Common questions: tilstandsgrad (TG), how borettslag/sameie work, financing.
- Uncertainty: TG, and "nobody dares to bid first" (herd behaviour).
- Would save most time if buyers read the salgsoppgave before asking.
- Note: the answers are mostly about used homes, not new-build projects.

## Data quality

- **To confirm with the group:** some unmarked write-ups are very complete and similar to each other (e.g. three young respondents with the same trade and near-identical concerns, and heading/number mismatches in one set). If any of them are not real interviews, the counts above change. See Questions in `docs/Status.md`.
- Several write-ups skip questions (budget, "after the interview"), so n varies per question.
- Sample skews to the Oslo area and to people who know the interviewers.
