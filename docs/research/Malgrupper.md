# Målgrupper (target groups)

- Job: buyer segments for Boligvelgeren, drawn from interview round 1. Evidence and IDs: `docs/research/Findings.md`.
- **Status: draft, Claude's interpretation (2026-11-02).** Not agreed by the group. Names and cut-offs are suggestions; change them freely.
- Segments are by situation and need, not by age alone. Each person sits in one segment; one outlier (R01, a young buy-to-let investor) fits none.

## Overview

| Segment | n | Who | Core question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Utforskeren | 7 | First-time, early stage, mostly mobile | "Hva har jeg råd til, i måneden?" |
| Sammenligneren | 5 | First-time, financing in place, comparing many units | "Hvorfor koster denne 300 000 mer?" |
| Familielogistikeren | 5 | Families with children, upgrading | "Får vi plass til alt, og når kan vi flytte?" |
| Livsfase-skifteren | 3 | 50+, selling a house or starting a new chapter | "Er dette trygt og riktig for resten av livet?" |

Livsfase-skifteren rests on only three interviews. Treat it as a signal to check, not a finding.

## Utforskeren (the explorer)

R03 R07 R10 R11 R14 R18 R20

- **Situation:** first-time buyer, renting or living at home, scrolling Finn on the bus or at night. Not yet talked to the bank or a megler.
- **Ranks:** price first (mean 1.4), then floor plan. Thinks in monthly cost, not purchase price.
- **Pains:** jargon (fellesgjeld, omkostninger, BRA); doesn't know what a good price is; PDFs unreadable on mobile; scared to call the megler; forms lead to sales calls.
- **Trusts:** parents (screenshots sent on Messenger). Positive to a chatbot because "I don't feel stupid".
- **Quote:** "Vi som kjøper første gang vet ikke hva vi ikke vet." (R14)
- **Needs:** monthly-cost view, plain-language explanations, a step-by-step guide, works on mobile, can read without leaving contact details.

## Sammenligneren (the comparer)

R02 R04 R05 R08 R09

- **Situation:** first-time buyer with financing, actively viewing. Several lost bidding rounds and turned to new builds because there is a fixed price (R05 R08 R09).
- **Ranks:** price (1.2), floor plan, then sun and balcony. Learns during the search that light and storage matter.
- **Pains:** clicking through 30 units one by one; can't link the PDF price list to the floor plan; sun per unit unknown; unexplained price gaps between near-identical units; parking and tilvalg hidden in small print.
- **Compares in:** screenshots and notes, or a spreadsheet typed in by hand.
- **Quote:** "Skjermbilder. Masse skjermbilder." (R05)
- **Needs:** side-by-side comparison, price explained per unit (floor, sun, view), sun for this unit, total price incl. parking, a shareable shortlist.

## Familielogistikeren (the family logistician)

R06 R13 R15 R19 R21

- **Situation:** owns already, needs more room for children. Two adults decide, often on separate screens at different times.
- **Ranks:** floor plan (1.4) and price, then storage/parking (3.8) and noise. View and floor barely matter.
- **Pains:** can't filter on bedrooms or storage size; "bod medfølger" with no size or location; school district, traffic and future building not on the project site; delivery date must fit selling the old home and changing kindergarten.
- **Compares in:** spreadsheets with weights, shared Google docs.
- **Quote:** "Vi er to som bestemmer, og vi ser på ting på hver vår telefon." (R15)
- **Needs:** filters (bedrooms, storage, parking), storage and parking shown in the building model, surroundings and planned development, a list two people can share and comment on, clear forbehold and timeline.

## Livsfase-skifteren (the life-stage mover)

R12 R16 R17

- **Situation:** 50+, often selling a house to fund a flat, buying "for the rest of my life" or "something of my own". Budget is less tight.
- **Ranks:** price low (mean 5.0); outdoor space, floor plan, noise and sun high.
- **Pains:** marketing instead of facts; small grey text; "meld interesse" everywhere when they just want to read; accessibility (lift to the garage, step-free access) not stated; neighbouring plots unknown.
- **Trusts:** people more than websites. A chatbot gets one chance: "hvis den svarer feil én gang, bruker jeg den ikke igjen" (R16).
- **Quote:** "Prosjektsidene er laget for å selge en livsstil, ikke for å svare på spørsmål." (R12)
- **Needs:** readable text, facts on build quality and accessibility, project risk and progress, a plan for selling the current home, easy route to a human.

## What all four share

- Price and floor plan rank top in every segment except Livsfase-skifteren.
- Nobody will share income or equity. Most will share needs and a budget range.
- Everyone wants to understand why units in the same building cost differently.
- A chatbot is accepted mainly for facts with a visible source.
