# Sprint routine (DRAFT)

- Job: how our one-week sprints and their events are meant to run. **Draft, not yet agreed by the group.**
- Source: Drive "Relevante Docs/Sprint meeting structures" (author not recorded), condensed on 2026-10-26. Course rules for the events are in `docs/course/CourseReference.md` section 7; this file covers only our choices.

## Before the sprints start

1. Agree on the project goal.
2. Agree on product ideas (see `Ideas.md`).
3. Agree on a Definition of Done (applies to all backlog items), and write acceptance criteria per item. Wording agreed by the user 2026-10-26; the Drive author will update the Drive document.

## Week shape

| When | Event | Timebox (draft) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Sprint planning | max 2 h |
| Every day, same time (09:00?) | Daily scrum | max 15 min |
| Friday | Sprint review | max 1 h |
| Friday, after a 15-min break | Sprint retrospective (Scrum team only) | max 45 min |

## Per event (from the draft)

- **Planning:** remind the team of the product goal. Ask why the sprint is valuable, what can be done, and how. Agree the sprint goal. Pick backlog items and break them into tasks. Consider the last increment, capacity and improvement actions.
- **Daily scrum:** progress toward the sprint goal, what's going well and what's harder than expected. Output: an adapted sprint backlog and a plan until the next daily.
- **Review:** developers, PO, SM and relevant stakeholders. Show what increments were made, demo the latest increment, gather feedback, adjust backlog priorities. Ends once the backlog is adapted.
- **Retrospective:** collaboration, tools, process and the DoD. Write down what went well and what could be better, then discuss improvements for next sprint.

## Fit with the course

- Review max 1 h matches the course figure of ~1 h per sprint week [L4 slide 23].
- Retro 45 min: the course gives 90 min for a two-week sprint and nothing for one week [L4 slide 30; CourseReference §10]. Halving it is a reasonable reading [interp].
- Planning max 2 h: the course's 4–8 h is for 2–4-week sprints [L2 pp. 26–30]; 2 h for one week is in proportion [interp].
- **Correction:** the draft says "DoD's for product backlog items". The course has one DoD for the whole increment; each item gets its own *acceptance criteria* [L2 pp. 10–13]. The draft also says "project owner" where it means Product Owner.
- The planning questions (why / what / how) and the daily-scrum wording match the Scrum Guide, which is not part of the course material. L1 frames the daily as yesterday / today / blockers [L1 p. 32]. Either works.
- Retro minutes must be submitted for every retrospective [L4 slide 45]. The draft doesn't mention this. Add it before agreeing.
